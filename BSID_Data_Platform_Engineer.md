# Job Title: BSID Data Platform Engineer

## Job Responsibility:
TSMC IT is actively seeking talented Data Platform Engineers to design, develop, and scale next-generation data platforms. This role sits at the intersection of data engineering, software engineering, and site reliability engineering (SRE) — you will build and optimize data pipelines, develop platform services as software products, and ensure they run reliably at scale. We are building a world-class, on-premises data platform on a cloud-native technology stack (Kubernetes, containerized services, GitOps) that empowers development teams across the organization to build data-driven applications on top of a reliable, scalable, and well-governed data infrastructure. Data governance is engineered into the platform rather than added on top of it — consistent business definitions, controlled access, and trustworthy lineage are what make enterprise data safe for AI applications to reason and act on. If you share our enthusiasm for data-driven and AI-empowered solutions, we'd love to hear from you.

## Your responsibilities include:
1. Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines for batch and real-time processing, and optimize data storage solutions to ensure data quality, reliability, and accessibility.
2. Develop data platform capabilities that enhance BI, AI/ML analytics, and reporting, enabling both human users and AI-powered applications to leverage enterprise data effectively.
3. Contribute to the design and development of a Semantic Layer service that enables AI Agents and AI-powered applications to discover, understand, and query enterprise data in a consistent and governed manner.
4. Build AI-integrated Data Engineering tools that streamline pipeline development, data transformation, and operational workflows through intelligent automation.
5. Develop and maintain an Internal Developer Platform (IDP) that provides self-service capabilities, standardized toolchains, and intuitive interfaces to improve developer experience.
6. Design well-structured APIs, backend services, and platform components following software engineering best practices, including comprehensive testing. Leverage agentic CLI tools and AI coding assistants to accelerate development and improve team productivity.
7. Apply SRE practices — including capacity planning, incident management, and performance tuning — to ensure reliability and performance of high-throughput query engines and workflow orchestration layers.
8. Define and track SLOs/SLIs for critical platform services, and implement monitoring, alerting, and logging to proactively detect and resolve issues.
9. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data requirements and deliver solutions that meet business needs. Ensure data governance, security, and compliance across the platform.

## Job Requirements
1. Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Engineering, or a similar technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
2. 4+ years of production-level experience as a Data Engineer, Software Engineer, SRE, or in a similar role focusing on data platform or infrastructure development. We look for senior-level capability — ownership of production systems, sound design judgment, and the ability to drive ambiguous problems to a decision
3. Strong understanding of database systems (SQL/NoSQL), data warehousing concepts, data modeling, and schema design. Familiarity with object storage and open table formats (e.g., S3-compatible storage, Delta Lake, Iceberg) is a plus.
4. Proficiency in Python or Java with a strong emphasis on writing optimized and maintainable code for data processing and platform services.
5. Solid software engineering fundamentals, including API design (REST/GraphQL), system design, version control, and code review practices.
6. Familiar with DevOps practices including CI/CD toolchains, quality control processes, and artifact management. Working knowledge of Kubernetes and containerized deployment.
7. Experience maintaining and scaling core data infrastructure components (e.g., Airflow, Trino, ClickHouse). Familiarity with DataHub, dbt, policy-based access control (e.g., Open Policy Agent), or identity and access management (OAuth 2.0 / OIDC) is a plus.
8. Familiar with SRE principles, including SLOs/SLIs, capacity planning, incident response, and observability stacks (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, ELK/OpenSearch).
9. Familiarity with streaming and messaging systems (e.g., Kafka, NATS), API gateway technologies, or building interfaces for AI agent consumption (e.g., MCP) is a plus.
